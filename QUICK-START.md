# 📝 Quick Start Guide — Adding Your Poems

Welcome! This website is your personal poetry archive. You don't need to know how to code — everything can be done directly in your browser.

## ✨ How to Add a New Poem

1. **Click the `+` button** in the bottom-right corner of any page (it's a round burgundy button that floats on the screen).

2. **Fill in the form:**
   - **Title**: The name of your poem
   - **Type**: Select "Poem" from the dropdown
   - **Date**: Pick today's date (or when you wrote it)
   - **Excerpt**: A short preview — the first line of your poem works great
   - **Content**: Paste or type your poem here
     - **Important**: Every line break will be preserved exactly as you type it
     - Leave blank lines between stanzas
     - Type it exactly how you want people to read it
   - **Tags**: Add keywords separated by commas (e.g., `friendship, gratitude, life`)
   - **Reading Time**: Leave as "2 min read" or change if needed
   - **Collection**: Choose "Personal", "Reflections", or "Moments"
   - **Featured**: Check this box if you want it to appear on the home page

3. **Click "Save Writing"** — done! Your poem appears instantly across the entire website.

## ✏️ How to Edit or Delete a Poem

1. **Open the poem** by clicking on it from the Archive or Home page.
2. **Look in the top-right corner** — you'll see two small buttons:
   - **Pencil icon** = Edit
   - **Trash icon** = Delete
3. Click **Edit** to change anything, or **Delete** to remove it.

## 💾 Backing Up Your Poems

It's a good idea to save a backup occasionally:

1. Go to the **About** page (click "About" in the navigation).
2. Scroll to the bottom.
3. Click **Export Archive (.json)** to download a backup file.
4. Save this file somewhere safe (Google Drive, email to yourself, etc.).

If you ever switch computers or browsers, just click **Import Archive (.json)** and upload that file — all your poems will be restored instantly.

## 📌 Important Notes

- **Your poems are saved in your browser** (in something called `localStorage`).
- They persist across sessions — they won't disappear when you close the browser.
- **But**: If you clear your browser's data or switch devices, you'll need to import your backup.
- That's why it's important to export a backup regularly!

## 🎨 Tips for Writing Poems

- **Line breaks matter**: The website preserves every line break exactly.
- **Stanzas**: Leave a blank line between stanzas (press Enter twice).
- **Tags**: Use lowercase tags like `friendship`, `nostalgia`, `midnight`, `nature`.
- **Excerpt**: Make it compelling — it's the first thing people see on cards.
- **Featured**: Check this for your favorite poems so they appear on the home page.

## 🌙 Dark Mode

Toggle between light and dark mode using the moon/sun icon in the navigation bar (top right). The site remembers your preference.

---

That's it! You're ready to start archiving your poetry. If you get stuck, just experiment — you can always edit or delete anything you create.

Happy writing! ✦
