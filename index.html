Companion & Guide

Special Features & Instructions

A guide to the tools, ambient audio, aesthetic postcard exporters, and collaborative workflows built into The Archive.

🎧
Audio & Ambience

Web Audio Ambient Soundscapes

Immerse yourself in gentle synthesized rainfall, cozy fireplace crackles, rolling ocean waves, forest breeze, or a flowing mountain stream while reading and writing. Powered entirely in the browser using the Web Audio API without needing external audio files.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Look at the top-right of the navigation bar for the Headphone icon.
  2. 2 Click to open the soundscape menu.
  3. 3 Select 🌧 Rain, 🔥 Fireplace, 🌊 Ocean Waves, 🍃 Forest Breeze, or 💧 Gentle Stream.
  4. 4 Use the volume slider to set your comfortable audio level. A glowing indicator dot signals sound is active.
📖
Reading Experience

Floating “Aa” Reader Customizer

Personalize your reading atmosphere with typography controls, scaling, margin widths, and a dedicated stanza focus spotlight.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Open any poem or writing piece to enter reading mode.
  2. 2 Click the floating Aa button in the bottom right corner.
  3. 3 Typefaces: Switch between Serif (Cormorant Garamond), Sans (Inter), or Mono (JetBrains Mono).
  4. 4 Text Scaling & Width: Scale font size with A- and A+, and adjust column width to Narrow, Medium, or Wide.
  5. 5 Stanza Focus Mode: Toggle the checkbox to dim all other lines and spotlight only the stanza you are currently hovering or reading.
Open Reading Mode →
🎨
Graphic Generator

Aesthetic Literary Postcard Exporter

Generate 1080×1080 high-resolution literary graphic cards complete with decorative double borders, author insignias, wax seals, and curated typography.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Open any poem in reading mode and click 🖼️ Postcard in the bottom toolbar.
  2. 2 Choose your theme: Warm Parchment, Midnight Obsidian, Crimson Velvet, or Sage Herbal.
  3. 3 Select a specific stanza or teaser quote from the excerpt dropdown.
  4. 4 Click Download PNG to save to your device or Copy Image to paste directly into chat messages.
Try Postcard Generator →
💬
Collaboration

Connected Poems & Literary Dialogues

Create bidirectional poetic conversations. Write in response to each other's pieces and build a web of literary dialogues across the archive.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Click the floating + write button.
  2. 2 Go through Step 1 (Details) and Step 2 (Write Canvas) to Step 3: Options.
  3. 3 In the “In Response To” dropdown, pick an existing piece by Neerav or Avigna.
  4. 4 When published, your piece will display a top dialogue banner linking to the original, and the original will showcase your response in its gallery.
✒️
Storytelling

Marginalia & Secret Author Notes

Capture the hidden memories, late-night backstory, or dedication behind any piece without cluttering the main poem.

How to Use:

  1. 1 When creating or editing a piece, navigate to Step 3: Options.
  2. 2 Add your thoughts into the “Behind the Lines / Marginalia” field.
  3. 3 When reading, an interactive [ ✒ Behind the Lines / Marginalia ] button appears under the poem.
  4. 4 Readers can click it to slide open the author's secret commentary drawer.
✦
Organization

Bookmarks & Favorites Shelf

Curate your personal collection of favorite writings across the archive with instant one-click bookmarking.

How to Use:

  1. 1 On any card in the Archive or Home page, click the ✦ star icon in the top right.
  2. 2 While reading any piece, click the ✦ Favorite button in the bottom floating toolbar.
  3. 3 Go to the Archive tab and click the ✦ Favorites filter button to view all your starred pieces.
View Favorites Shelf →
🪶
Writing Studio

Distraction-Free Zen Fullscreen Mode

A serene, full-screen writing sanctuary with zero interface distractions and real-time word and line counts.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Click + Write and complete Step 1 (Title, Author, Date).
  2. 2 In Step 2 (Canvas), click 🪶 Zen Mode in the top right.
  3. 3 Enjoy a distraction-free writing screen with live word counts.
  4. 4 Press Esc or click Exit Zen to return to the modal and publish your work.
💾
Data Protection

Draft Auto-Saving & Instant Recovery

Never worry about lost poems or accidental browser closures. Your writing is continuously auto-saved locally in real time.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Whenever you type in the writing canvas, your work is saved automatically.
  2. 2 If you close your browser tab or restart your computer, simply click + Write.
  3. 3 A gold banner will appear: “Unsaved draft found from [timestamp]”.
  4. 4 Click Restore Draft to bring back all your text, title, tags, and settings.
🕯️
Insignias & Identity

Author Signature Wax Seals

Distinguished author emblems stamped at the conclusion of every poem and literary piece.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Neerav: Stamped with the Crimson & Gold Archivist seal (N) — “Written with care by Neerav · The Archivist”.
  2. 2 Avigna: Stamped with the Amethyst & Star Muse seal (✦) — “Written with heart by Avigna · The Muse”.
  3. 3 Simply select the author name when publishing, and the seal is automatically generated.

Security & Privacy

Dual-Password Gatekeeper & Instant Lock

Protect your private writings with a secure gatekeeper screen and two secret passphrase keys.

How to Use:

  1. 1 Passphrases: Unlock as Neerav using friendship or as Avigna using terrible judgement.
  2. 2 Quick Lock: Click the Padlock icon in the top navigation bar anytime to immediately lock the archive and clear your session.
  3. 3 Backup & Restore: Go to the About page to export or import your entire archive as a single .json file.
